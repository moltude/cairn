CalTopo → onX Backcountry

Move your map without losing its icons, colors or folders.

Cairn reads a CalTopo export, shows you every waypoint and track the way onX will see it, lets you fix what won't carry over, then writes files onX can import.

  1. 1In CalTopo, export the map as GeoJSON.
  2. 2Drop it here and review icons and colors.
  3. 3Export, then import the files into onX following the included steps.

Your map never leaves this computer — everything runs in your browser.
